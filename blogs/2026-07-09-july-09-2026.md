Feature Generally Available : You can now configure separate, granular access permissions for discovering and joining a space in the Google Chat API . To specify who can discover or join a space, include the AccessSettings object in the spaces resource, and use the accessPermissionSettings field to specify granular permissions. For more information, see the Target audience for the Spaces resource .
