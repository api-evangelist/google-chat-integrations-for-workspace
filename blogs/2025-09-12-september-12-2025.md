Feature Generally available: Increased per-space quota limits for writing messages and reactions with the Chat API: When importing data to Google Chat, the per-space quota limit for writing messages with spaces.messages.create increases from 60 to 600 per minute. When writing reactions with spaces.messages.reactions.create, the per-space quota limit increases from 60 to 300 per minute. To learn more, see the Chat API's per-space quotas.
